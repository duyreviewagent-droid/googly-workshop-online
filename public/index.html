HOW TO PLAY

You own a grocery store. A month is 30 days and each day lasts one minute. Whoever is richest after 30 days wins (cash + stock in your stockroom).

1. Stock is automatic. AUTO-ORDER (on by default, in Buy Stock, B) keeps your stockroom full, and your stocker carries boxes to the shelves. You can still order yourself and carry trolleys (E at the stockroom rack) when it's busy.

2. Sell more kinds of food. New shelves, fridges and freezers (U) come with products already picked; auto-order and your stocker fill them.

3. Ring up shoppers. Stand behind the till and hold E to scan. Or hire a cashier (U) and let them do it.

4. Set prices (P). Charge more and you earn more on each sale, but shoppers grumble "too pricey!" and go to rival stores. Watch what the others charge.

5. Grow (U). Buy more shelves, fridges and freezers so you sell more kinds of food. Hire staff, open a second checkout lane, run ads. Mop up spills (hold E), because messy stores lose stars.

Every morning there's news. A heatwave means ice cream sells fast, and an egg shortage means eggs cost more to buy but sell for more too. Fresh food spoils a little every night, so don't overbuy.
